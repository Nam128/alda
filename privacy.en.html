Alda Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alda (the “App”) handles your information and how you can reach us with privacy questions or concerns. The App’s developer is an individual based in the Republic of Korea, and this policy is established and published in accordance with Article 30 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act so that your personal information is protected and related concerns are handled promptly and smoothly.

1. Information we process

The App has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The decks, cards, study history, daily goals, and settings you create while using the App, and any photos you attach to cards, are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer’s servers or to any third party. The developer cannot access this information.

The App is free and uses the advertising SDK of Google LLC (Google AdMob) to show ads. When it loads ads, the ad SDK sends device information and other data to Google. Sections 3 and 5 describe what is sent and how to opt out.

If you send us an email through Settings > Contact us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message (including the app version, device type, OS version, and app error log, if you leave them in the message) and use them only to respond to that inquiry.

2. How long we keep information

Information stored on your device stays on that device until you delete it in the App or delete the App.

Inquiry emails are deleted one year after the day we finish responding.

3. Disclosure through the ad SDK and international transfers

The developer does not itself provide your personal information to third parties or outsource its processing. However, to show ads, the Google advertising SDK in the App sends the following information to Google.

Recipient: Google LLC (contact: through the channels listed in Google’s Privacy Policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Destination countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates servers

When and how: sent over the internet each time the App loads an ad or an ad is displayed or clicked

Information transferred: device information (device model, OS version, language), IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location), a device identifier scoped to the app developer (IDFV), ad interaction records (ad impressions and clicks), diagnostic data from the ad SDK (errors and performance), and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA)

Purposes: serving ads and measuring their performance, preventing invalid clicks, and (if you allow tracking) showing ads based on your interests

Retention period: as set out in Google’s Privacy Policy

How to refuse and what happens if you do: You can refuse to provide the advertising identifier (IDFA) as described in Section 5, and every feature of the App remains available if you do. The other information is required to load ads, so if you do not want it sent, stop using the App and delete it; nothing further will be sent.

Apart from the ad SDK, the App does not use any third-party SDKs for analytics or tracking.

4. Use of device features

Photos (optional): When you add a photo to a card, only the photo you choose is copied into the App’s storage. All other features work without adding photos.

Read aloud: Text is read aloud with the iOS voices built into your device, and the text is not sent off the device.

Dictation: Keyboard dictation is handled by iOS, and Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to it.

Home Screen widgets: Card content is exchanged with the widgets through App Group storage on the same device.

Apple Watch (optional): Today’s review cards and your ratings are exchanged directly with your paired Apple Watch over Apple’s device-to-device connection (WatchConnectivity), without passing through the developer’s servers.

Error log: If an error freezes the App’s screen, the App keeps the details of up to the 20 most recent errors on your device. This log is included in an email only when you send it yourself through Contact us, and is otherwise never sent off the device.

App Lock (optional): To hide the apps you choose, the App uses the iOS Screen Time permission (Family Controls). The apps you choose are stored on your device only as opaque tokens provided by Apple. The App does not collect usage records such as app names or screen time, and does not send them off the device. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Listening review: So that a listening session you started can continue while the screen is off, the App temporarily creates audio files on your device and deletes them when the session ends.

5. Automatic collection and behavioral information

The App does not use cookies.

Advertising identifier (IDFA): In versions with ads, after the App’s first screen opens and before the ad SDK starts, the App asks for your permission through the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt. The prompt does not appear again if you have already made a choice or have restricted tracking requests in your device settings. Only if you allow tracking does the Google ad SDK use the advertising identifier and behavioral information, such as how you respond to ads in other apps and on other websites, to show ads based on your interests. If you do not allow it, ads are shown without personalization, and all other features of the App remain available.

You can turn tracking on or off at any time on your device in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or in Settings > Alda.

However, if you have turned on Share With App Developers in your device settings, Apple may provide the developer with crash logs and usage statistics in a form that does not identify you. You can turn this off at any time on your device in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

6. Deleting information

Running Settings > Erase all history in the App immediately deletes the study history and attached photos stored on your device, and they cannot be recovered. When you delete the App, iOS deletes all of the App’s storage.

Files created with Settings > Export backup are saved to the location you choose (such as the Files app or Messages) and are subject to that location’s policies. You must delete these files yourself.

7. Your rights

You can view, edit, and delete your records in the App at any time. The only information the developer keeps is the inquiry emails you send; you can request access to or deletion of them by emailing the privacy officer listed in Section 10.

For information sent by the Google ad SDK, you can exercise your rights with Google in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

8. Children’s privacy

The App does not ask for your age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The App requests only ads suitable for all ages (Google ad content rating G). Parents and guardians can keep tracking turned off as described in Section 5.

9. Security measures

Information stored on your device is protected by iOS app isolation (sandboxing) and Data Protection, and if you set a device passcode, it is kept encrypted while the device is locked.
